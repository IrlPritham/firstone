My Dream Build

I'd build my dream 765RS to be clean, dark, and practical for everyday riding.

A matte black or deep grey wrap with minimal decals to keep it stealthy.

Softer seat foam and a wider pillion seat so my wife feels comfortable on longer rides.

Raised handlebars just a bit, for a relaxed posture in city traffic.

Slightly tuned suspension—not track stiff, but smooth over Indian roads.

Full-system exhaust, but not too loud, because peace matters on daily rides.

Better mirrors and subtle LED indicators for a neat look.

Tank grips for control, especially when riding with my wife.

Good quality tyres that handle rain, heat, and bad roads well.

A bike that looks aggressive, rides easy, and keeps both of us happy every day.
